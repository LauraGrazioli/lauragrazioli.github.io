I am Laura Grazioli and I am currently a Postdoc at [CERMICS](https://cermics-lab.enpc.fr), Ecole nationale des ponts et chaussées, Marne-la-Vallée, France.

My research focuses on electronic structure theory with

- wave function theory
- tools from differential geometry
- unitary coupled cluster theory

On this website you can find information about my research, publications, invited talks, and professional activities.
